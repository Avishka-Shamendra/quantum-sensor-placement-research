Its mean greedy-to-optimal sensor ratio was 1.130 (95% stratified-bootstrap CI 1.129--1.132); the 95th percentile was 1.455.

For the 2D benchmark (n = 19800), the mean ratio was 1.124 and the exact-match rate was 41.0%.

These results describe the stated normalized synthetic benchmark distribution. They do not strengthen the worst-case guarantee or establish performance for every physical sensing application.
